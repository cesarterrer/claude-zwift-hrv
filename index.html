Claude-Zwift HRV

A personal training-data tool. Single user. Not a public service.

What it does

It reads the owner's own heart-rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep duration from the Google Health API and writes them into the owner's own intervals.icu training log, so that recovery data and cycling training data sit in one place.

Who it is for

One person: the developer, using it on their own account and their own data. There are no other users, no sign-ups and no shared data.
